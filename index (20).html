Startime Studio is not a salon — it is an atelier. Nestled in the Žižkov quarter of Prague, we have built a sanctuary where the science of cosmetic chemistry meets the patience of fine craft.

"Every strand is a canvas. Every visit, a composition."

We reject the conveyor-belt model. Each appointment begins with a thorough consultation — hair history, lifestyle, skin undertone analysis — so the result is not just beautiful, but authentically yours.
